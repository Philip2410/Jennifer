What it is

A Clear Look at Your Risks Before Anyone Talks Products

Most people buy insurance in pieces: some through the bank, a group plan at work, an old policy from a relative. Nobody has ever added it up.

Jennifer Chambers does. She looks at your goals, income, debts, family and current coverage, then shows you the gaps. Sometimes the answer is life insurance. Sometimes it's nothing new, just a better fit for what you already pay for.

Want a rough number first? Try her Insurance Needs Calculator or read Planning for Your Financial Future.

What you get

What Your Grande Prairie Needs Analysis Covers

  • A Real Number

    How much coverage your family or business needs, worked out from your own debts, income and goals.

  • Gaps Found Early

    Work benefits that end with the job, or mortgage insurance that only pays the bank. Better found now than at claim time.

  • One First Step

    Not a list of ten products. You'll know what matters first and what can wait.

  • No Judgment

    Money is emotional. Ask anything, and expect a plan that leaves room to enjoy today too.
