Jennifer Chambers Financial Services ("we", "us") is an insurance practice in Grande Prairie, Alberta. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what we do with it. We follow Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

This policy covers the website only. When you apply for insurance or an investment product, the insurance company collects information too, and its own privacy policy also applies.

What We Collect

When You Contact Us
If you fill in the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the service you're interested in and your message.

When You Book a Meeting
If you book through our online booking page, the booking tool collects your name, contact details and the time you choose.

When You Call or Email
We keep the details you give us so we can reply and follow up.

When You Visit the Site
We use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits, see which pages are read, and see when someone taps the phone number, email link or sends the form. This tool doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect information that identifies you personally.

We don't ask for health, financial or other sensitive information through this website. Please don't include it in the contact form. If it's needed, we'll ask for it in a meeting or through the insurance company's secure application.

Why We Collect It

  • To answer your questions and set up meetings
  • To follow up about the services you asked about
  • To understand how the website is used, so we can improve it

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. We don't use it for anything you wouldn't reasonably expect from the reason you gave it to us.

Your Consent

By sending the form, booking a meeting or contacting us, you agree to us using your information for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, and we'll stop using your information for those purposes unless the law requires us to keep it.

Who We Share It With

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the website and our practice, and only as much as they need:

  • Website hosting and forms: Netlify, Inc. (United States)
  • Website analytics: a privacy-friendly analytics tool run by our web designer
  • Booking, email and client management: the online tools we use to schedule meetings, send email and keep track of enquiries

If you decide to apply for a product, your information is shared with the insurance company you choose, with your permission.

Storage Outside Canada

Some of our service providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States. Information stored outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country, and may be accessed by its courts or authorities. If you have questions about how our service providers outside Canada handle your information, contact our privacy contact below.

How Long We Keep It

We keep contact form messages and booking details only as long as we need them to respond to you and follow up, or as long as the law or our professional obligations require. Then we delete them securely.

How We Protect It

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections, password-protected accounts and limiting access to the people who need it. If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify you and the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta as PIPA requires.

Your Rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask how it's been used. Send your request to our privacy contact below. We'll respond within the time PIPA allows (45 days, in most cases).

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

Links to Other Websites

This site links to other websites, such as Jennifer's article and calculator site, social media and the organizations she belongs to. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
