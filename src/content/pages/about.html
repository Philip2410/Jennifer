Her story

From Optician to Insurance Advisor in Grande Prairie

Jennifer Chambers was an optician for 25 years. A financial wake-up call in her 30s changed that, and became the reason for a second career: helping people avoid the same path.

Today she owns Jennifer Chambers Financial Services, with five-plus years in insurance and twenty in the Grande Prairie community. Her meetings start with listening, not a product, because she knows how it feels to not like your own numbers.

What she focuses on

Insurance and Planning for Grande Prairie Families and Business Owners

Jennifer Chambers is independent and works with several insurance companies, so she compares options instead of selling one company's product.

  • Life Insurance

    Term and whole life coverage, built around your budget, how long you need it and what you want it to do.

  • Disability & Critical Illness

    Income protection if an accident or illness stops you working, or a lump sum after a covered diagnosis.

  • Small Business Benefits

    Benefits and key person coverage for teams of 1 to 10, plus succession plans for family businesses.

  • Savings & Investments

    Segregated funds and other insured investments for growth with added protection.
