Your options

Term and Whole Life Insurance in Grande Prairie

There's no single best policy. It depends on what matters most: a low monthly cost, coverage for life, or cash value that grows.

  • Term Life Insurance

    Coverage for a set number of years, while the mortgage is big and the kids are young. Lowest cost to start, but pricier at each renewal.

  • Whole Life Insurance (Participating)

    Lifetime coverage, guaranteed cash value, level premiums. Dividends can grow your coverage. More: Insurance for Your Whole Life.

Mortgage insurance

Mortgage Life Insurance You Control, Not the Bank

The bank's mortgage insurance is quick to say yes to. A policy you own works differently.

Bank mortgage insurance compared with a life insurance policy you own
Bank's insurance A policy you own
Who Gets Paid The bank The people you choose
The Payout Shrinks as your balance drops Stays at the amount you picked
Moving Lenders Usually stays behind Comes with you

Not the same as CMHC mortgage default insurance, which protects the lender. Pair it with disability or critical illness cover so payments get made if you're sick or hurt.

Life Insurance FAQs in Grande Prairie

  • Enough to cover your debts, replace your income for as long as your family would need it, and pay for big goals like your kids' education. Everyone's number is different. Try the Insurance Needs Calculator for a rough idea, then Jennifer Chambers can check it against your real situation.

  • It depends on your budget and goals. Term costs less while your kids are young and your mortgage is big. Whole life costs more but lasts your whole life and builds cash value. Many families use both: term for the big years, whole life for lasting coverage and a legacy.

  • Group life insurance through work usually ends or drops when you leave, retire or are laid off. Some plans let you convert it, often at a higher cost. A personal policy you own stays in place no matter where you work, which is why many people keep one alongside their work coverage.
